Most managed service providers are sales organisations with an engineering department attached. We built the inverse.

BrnDynamics started in Karachi in 2019 with a small group of engineers who had spent their careers inside the estates they now look after. That is still the hiring bar, and Karachi is still where the build and the service desk live.

Geneva came next, because European clients need European contracting, European data-protection terms and someone reachable in European hours. Riyadh followed for the same reason in the Gulf, where data residency and Vision 2030 programmes need people on the ground rather than on a call.

The three offices are not a sales map. They are a working day that starts in Karachi, overlaps with Riyadh through the morning and Geneva through the afternoon, and hands back. That is what lets us answer at 03:00 with an engineer instead of an apology.

Every engagement is scoped by the engineer who will own it, and the same pod stays with your estate for the life of the contract. No tiered queue designed to keep you from the people who can fix the problem, and no account manager translating your incident into a ticket category. It costs us margin on paper. It is also why clients who joined in our first year are still here.
